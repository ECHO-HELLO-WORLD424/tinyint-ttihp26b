win0 (256 cycles) counts fit 16 bits at every corner for both canaries: **yes**. win2 also fits everywhere; win3 would wrap ro_gen at the fast/typ corners (`sat_win3` is the model's overflow-risk flag, not an RTL saturation flag) and is not a usable single-window readout there.

## One-point calibration (placeholder rows)

Each predictor x is calibrated at the primary nominal anchor (1.20 V, 25 C, seg3333, worst). The v2 protocol has no fallback anchor: if the primary boundary is censored above the 50 MHz ceiling, calibration is reported unavailable (`docs/prediction-model.md`). The rule is k_x = F_meas / P_x(anchor), applied as P_x_cal = k_x * P_x everywhere, unchanged. Pre-silicon every row carries `cal_k = 1.0` and `predicted_fmax_mhz_cal = predicted_fmax_mhz`; the post-silicon run substitutes the measured k only.

## Notes

- A FORCE_CAN-off SDF RO cross-check would be INVALID for model purposes: the RO loop cells are annotated with hard 0.000 SDF delays because `src/pnr.sdc` disables RO timing arcs, so its gen_cnt/mat_cnt are simulator race artifacts (`docs/ro-sdf-crosscheck-diagnosis.md`). It is excluded from this package; the DUT-boundary rows (FORCE_CAN on) are unaffected.
- The zero-delay reference row (ops=200, err=0) validates the testbench protocol, not timing.
- The STA knee is a linear slack translation (T_fail = T_clk - slack / duty, clock tree/uncertainty held at the corner); validity degrades far from the analyzed 20 ns point.
- Frame = 19 clk cycles (frame_cnt 0..18); one timed DUT operation per frame.
